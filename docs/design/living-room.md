# Living Room

Status: room colors and art are placeholders until the art direction is approved. Rules below are decided.

## Overview
The living room is the pet's home and the scene behind Today. Like the pet, it is separate from the app theme: switching light and dark changes the habit sheet and cards only. No live weather.

## Colors
Warm, cozy, and flat, in the same style as the pet. The window shows 4 times of day from the device's clock: morning, day, evening, night. Prototype 2 uses the day sky only. Every coat color must read clearly in the room at every time of day. The menu button and status bar keep 3:1 contrast against the room; the status bar follows the window's time of day.

## Typography
None. The room has no text besides the status bar.

## Layout
Full screen behind the habit sheet. The room is shifted up so the window sits just under the status bar. Room parts: front door (with the Pet Door after that quest), window with sill, couch, pet bed. The bed is cut into back and front layers so the pet can sit in it, and it sits inside the phone safe area (new bed art pending, P2-02). The pet's idle spots follow Confidence (see pet.md). Keep the pet's face clear of the habit sheet and the menu button.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat, no outlines, depth from darker same-color shapes. No glows.

## Shapes
Rounded, simple furniture that fits the pet's organic style. Home items from the shop (beds, room items) are placed in the room.

## Components
Window (4 times of day), front door, Pet Door, couch, pet bed (back and front layers), window sill, placed home items. Prototype 2 does not show the Pet Door. Small gags: a bird bumping the window, the pet doing a sudden spastic run. Activity Bar props: the food bowl slides in, a toy is tossed in.

## Do's and Don'ts
Do:
- Keep the room the same in every theme.
- Change it only with time of day.
- Check pet and menu contrast at every time of day.

Don't:
- Tie room colors to a theme.
- Add text to the room.
- Add live weather.
